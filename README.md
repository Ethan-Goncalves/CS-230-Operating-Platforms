# CS-230-Operating-Platforms
Briefly summarize The Gaming Room client and their software requirements.
The Gaming room was a client that wanted to expand their existing Android game, Draw it or Lose it onto a web based application which would make it accessible across platforms. For this particular need in the project it was recommending servers along with the best Operating systems for the servers that they were going to host. A few other important details come from having and managing unique identifiers along with maintain instances of the game simultaneously.

What did you do particularly well in developing this documentation?
Something I believe I did particularly well was using Lucid chart to construct the UML class design in a visual way that made it easy to read and understand. Learning and using lucid chart has made me appreciate making visual elements to showcase design wants.


What about the process of working through a design document did you find helpful when developing the code?

Working through a design document helped me realize that using something like that helps keep priorities on track and organized. Usually when I code I tackle various aspects in random orders but with this design document it made it easy to build on and tackle.


 If you could choose one part of your work on these documents to revise, what would you pick? How would you improve it?
 I would focus primarily on the recommendations especially with how easy it is to forget I am not designing these recommendations around my needs but it is the clients needs that matter most. When I was handing out my recommendations I thought of solutions that were really cost effective from my point of view but it is important to only consider the clients needs.

  How did you interpret the user's needs and implement them into your software design? Why is it important to consider the user's needs when designing?

I interpreted their needs as what will it take to get the game running in a state they are comfortable with and that meets all of the design requirements. The main consideration was making sure the game was scalable to user sizes and available on all platforms they wanted supported.

How did you approach designing software? What techniques or strategies would you use in the future to analyze and design a similar software application?

My approach was reviewing the clients design requirements and making sure I try my best to fulfill them and meet their expectations. A main concern was evaluating the various operating systems and if they would fulfill the needs.
